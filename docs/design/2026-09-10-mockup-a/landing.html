How it works

Three steps, then it keeps the books for you.

Step 1
Add your credential

Pick it from 170 source-linked templates or describe your own. iTrack lays out the cycle, the credit total, the minimums, and the caps, and asks you the conditional questions the board would.

Step 2
Log what you complete

A course, a conference, a webinar. Attach the certificate once and it counts toward every credential it qualifies for, without double-counting your total.

Step 3
Renew with the packet

When the deadline nears, print a cycle packet with exact balances, every activity, and an inventory of your evidence. Record the submission, then the acceptance, and the next cycle opens clean.

Who it's for

Anyone who renews a license or a certification.

Counselors, social workers, nurses, EMS, accountants, planners, safety and quality professionals, and 40 more profession labels across 98 issuing bodies. Seventy-nine templates are state-specific for California, Florida, New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, and Texas; the rest are national or global.

Why trust it
Rules link to their source
Every template cites the board's published requirements and shows when it was last reviewed. The board's current instructions always control.
Your evidence is private
Certificates and photos are stored for you alone. Nothing is shared, sold, or used for advertising.
An organizer, not an authority
iTrack never decides that a course is eligible or that a renewal is approved. It keeps the record so you can show your work.
Free, with no ads
Every feature is available to every account. Export your records at any time.
What happens after you sign up

Confirm your email, add one credential, log one course. Ten minutes.

You'll get a confirmation email from iTrack. Once you confirm, the app opens on an empty ledger with one prompt: add your first credential. Reminders are off until you turn them on.
